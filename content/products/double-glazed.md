## Overview
Shugg vertical sliding windows can be configured with double glazing to significantly enhance thermal performance, energy efficiency, and acoustic insulation. Ideal for modern residential and commercial builds, our double glazed units offer a contemporary, sashless appearance while maintaining superior insulation.

For projects requiring a minimalist look without the additional cost of full double glazing, Shugg windows can also achieve comparable insulation ratings through the use of advanced Low E (low emissivity) glass technology. This option retains the sleek sashless design and delivers impressive thermal performance.
