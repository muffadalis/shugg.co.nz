## Overview
The motorised Shugg vertical sliding window system brings automation and ease-of-use to all configurations — including Single Hung, Double Hung, and IGU (Insulated Glass Unit) options. Designed to meet modern expectations of convenience, accessibility, and smart control, the motorised version is ideal for high or difficult-to-reach locations, as well as for users seeking a more seamless lifestyle or commercial automation.

The integrated motor allows smooth and quiet operation via a remote. Despite the added technology, the window retains its hallmark sashless appearance, offering unobstructed views and refined minimalism.
