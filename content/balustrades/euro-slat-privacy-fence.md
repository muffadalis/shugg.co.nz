## Overview
Euro Slat screening is a recent innovation, developed due to demand for a high quality alternative to conventional high maintenance, low security, wooden trellis style screens. Produced from high-tensile T6 grade aluminium extrusions, you can be assured of many years of maintenance-free, high-security privacy screening that will enhance your property and lifestyle. With Euro Slat privacy screening, the possibilities are endless.
