## Overview
Our Frameless Glass Balustrade are at the forefront of balustrade safety and design. Our solutions are constructed from strong durable non weld quality T6106 aluminium and 1st Grade Standards Approved Safety glass. Unique styling to complement modern and contemporary NZ architect.
