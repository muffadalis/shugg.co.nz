## Overview
Euro Fin balustrade is the latest innovative product to the range of balustrade and privacy screening products.

Euro Fin can be top or side fixed. Euro Fin will appeal to Architects looking for bespoke façade decoration that blends seamlessly with a balustrade system.

Developed due to increased high density housing that has created demand for greater privacy while maintaining good visibility beyond your deck or outdoor living space. As well as extremely high security due to no climbing points.

Available in two profile options, including: 50×15 and 40×40 baluster profiles, that can be spaced close together for greater privacy or larger spacings to allow greater views. A truly versatile balustrade system.

Produced from high-tensile T6 grade aluminium non weld extrusions, you can be assured of many years of maintenance-free, high-security balustrade privacy screening that will enhance your property and lifestyle. With Euro Fin Balustrade, the possibilities are endless.
