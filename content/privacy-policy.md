**Effective Date:** June 3, 2025  

At **Shugg**, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you visit our website.

### 1. What We Collect

We collect non-personal information such as:

- Pages viewed, clicks, scrolls, and general interaction
- Device and browser details
- IP address for approximate geographic location

We do **not** collect personal identifiers unless you explicitly provide them.

### 2. Use of Microsoft Clarity

We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve and market our products/services. Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to determine the popularity of products/services and online activity. Additionally, we use this information for site optimization, fraud/security purposes, and advertising. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, visit the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

### 3. Use of Information

We use the data to:

- Improve website design and usability
- Monitor site performance
- Debug issues and optimize content

### 4. Cookies

We use cookies from Microsoft Clarity and other third parties. Cookies are used to:

- Track user behavior
- Remember preferences

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Some site features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

### 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell or rent your information. Aggregated data may be shared with analytics providers to improve the site.

### 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access or request deletion of your data
- Opt out of tracking via browser settings or privacy tools

### 7. External Links

Our website may link to other sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

### 8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date will be revised when changes are made.
