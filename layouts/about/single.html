The Shugg vertical sliding window system is a smart, modern solution designed for architects, joiners, and builders seeking unobstructed views and architectural flexibility. With its innovative sashless design and slim aluminium or timber profiles, the Shugg system blends seamlessly into a wide range of architectural styles.

Since 1984, Neotec has been committed to solving unique window and ventilation challenges. Our range of vertical window products can be customised to suit a variety of applications, and we actively collaborate with architects and joiners to bring design concepts to life with practical, engineered solutions.

In residential projects, the Shugg slider is widely recognised for providing both high and low-level ventilation without compromising views. Its ability to integrate into aluminium or timber doors makes it ideal for apartments and retrofit applications where improved airflow is essential.

In education settings, the demand for student safety and non-protruding openings has led to the Shugg slider becoming a preferred choice. Its smooth, wind-resistant operation prevents slamming, and our purpose-built school lock—designed for access across wide benches—makes it a practical and safe solution.

Our most dynamic growth has been in the commercial sector, where the Shugg balcony slider has become a standout feature in restaurants and cafés. The single slider system also supports a wide range of custom hatch solutions in glass, timber, whiteboards, and laminated finishes—pushing the boundaries of functional design.

At Neotec, we thrive on turning architectural ideas into reality. If you need advice on design details or custom solutions, our team is here to help. We're constantly evolving our product range to meet the demands of innovative building design.
