The 4 Panel Single Glazed Double Hung Vertical Sliding Window offers a classic aesthetic combined with practical functionality, making it ideal for both traditional and contemporary architectural styles.

Designed for enhanced ventilation, ease of operation, and minimal maintenance, this vertical sliding window is a versatile solution for residential and light commercial applications.

  • Features
  • Technical Drawings
  • Configurations
  • Applications
  • Double Hung Operation Both upper and lower sashes slide vertically, allowing for controlled ventilation and easy cleaning from inside the room.
  • 4 Panel Configuration Expands design possibilities and maximizes light entry while maintaining symmetry and balance.
  • Single Glazed Glass Lightweight and cost-effective, suitable for internal spaces or temperate climates where insulation is not a primary concern.
  • Smooth Sliding Mechanism Counterbalanced sashes ensure smooth and effortless operation over time, with minimal wear and tear.
  • Slimline Profiles Minimalist frame design enhances the glass-to-frame ratio, delivering a sleek, unobtrusive appearance.
  • Lockable Hardware Standard locking options enhance security and prevent unauthorized opening.
  • Customizable Finishes Available in a range of powder-coated finishes and hardware styles to complement any interior or exterior design.
